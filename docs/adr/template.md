# NNNN: Short decision title

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What situation or constraint forces a decision?

## Options

1. **Option A:** what it is, with its main advantage and drawback.
2. **Option B:** the same.

## Decision

What we chose, and why it beat the other options.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is still open.
